Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

BusinessQuotes uploader is used only by the BusinessQuotes channel owner, on their own computer, with their own accounts.

What the app accesses

  • TikTok: video upload (video.upload) to send the owner's own videos to their TikTok drafts.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) to the owner's own channel.
  • Facebook: permission to post Reels to the owner's own Page.

What is stored

Only the sign-in tokens needed to upload, kept locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared, or sent to anyone other than the platform being uploaded to. No data about other users or viewers is collected.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's own channel. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app stores only a sign-in token on the owner's computer and does not store, show or share any other YouTube data.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time in each platform's settings, which makes the stored tokens useless: Google/YouTube at myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok in Settings and privacy, Security, Apps; Facebook in Settings, Business integrations.

Questions: contact BusinessQuotes through the channel's profile on TikTok or YouTube.
