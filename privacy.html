Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

BusinessQuotes uploader is used only by the BusinessQuotes channel owner, on their own computer, with their own accounts.

What the app accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile info (user.info.basic) to confirm the signed-in account, and video upload (video.upload) to send the owner's own videos to their TikTok drafts.
  • YouTube and Facebook: permission to upload videos to the owner's own channel and Page.

What is stored

Only the sign-in tokens needed to upload, kept locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared, or sent to anyone other than the platform being uploaded to. No data about other users or viewers is collected.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time in each platform's settings (TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security, Apps), which makes the stored tokens useless.

Questions: contact BusinessQuotes through the channel's profile on TikTok or YouTube.
